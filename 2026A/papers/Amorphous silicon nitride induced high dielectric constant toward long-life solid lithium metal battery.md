## Abstract  摘要

Solid [polymer electrolytes](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/materials-science/polymer-electrolyte "Learn more about polymer electrolytes from ScienceDirect's AI-generated Topic Pages") are the most promising [solid electrolytes](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/materials-science/solid-electrolyte "Learn more about solid electrolytes from ScienceDirect's AI-generated Topic Pages") due to their mechanical flexibility and manufacturing scalability. However, the low lithium-ion transference number and [battery](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/engineering/battery-electrochemical-energy-engineering "Learn more about battery from ScienceDirect's AI-generated Topic Pages") failure with detrimental dendrites growth inhibit its commercial application in solid-state batteries. Here amorphous [silicon nitride](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/chemical-engineering/silicon-nitride "Learn more about silicon nitride from ScienceDirect's AI-generated Topic Pages") with high permittivity was introduced to both restrain the anion motion and screen the electric potential under [external electric field](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/engineering/external-electric-field "Learn more about external electric field from ScienceDirect's AI-generated Topic Pages"), by which the lithium-ion transference number was improved and the dendrite growth was inhibited significantly. The symmetric Li//Li cell paired with this solid polymer electrolyte exhibits a high lithium-ion transference number of 0.53, with excellent lithium plating/stripping capability at [high current density](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/engineering/high-current-density "Learn more about high current density from ScienceDirect's AI-generated Topic Pages") of 1.0 mA cm−2 over 250 h at room temperature. The practical application of this solid polymer electrolyte is verified by the capacity retention of 86.5% over 500 cycles and 70.5% even after 1000 cycles at room temperature with Li//LiFePO4 pouch cell at 1C. The [fire retardant](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/physics-and-astronomy/flame-retardant "Learn more about fire retardant from ScienceDirect's AI-generated Topic Pages") of this solid polymer electrolyte is demonstrated by an excellent self-extinguish behavior (< 1 s) in the flame test. Additionally, this solid polymer electrolyte system presents an effective strategy for enhancing anode interfacial stability for other [battery](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/engineering/battery-electrochemical-energy-engineering "Learn more about battery from ScienceDirect's AI-generated Topic Pages") systems.  
固态聚合物电解质由于其机械柔韧性和制造可扩展性，是最有前景的固态电解质。然而，锂离子迁移数低以及在外部电场下有害枝晶生长导致的电池失效限制了其在固态电池中的商业化应用。在此，引入了具有高介电常数的无定形硅氮化物，以限制阴离子运动并屏蔽外部电场下的电势，从而提高了锂离子迁移数并显著抑制了枝晶生长。与这种固态聚合物电解质配对的对称 Li//Li 电池在室温下以 1.0 mA cm⁻²的高电流密度循环 250 小时后，表现出 0.53 的高锂离子迁移数，具有出色的锂沉积/剥离能力。该固态聚合物电解质的实际应用通过室温下 Li//LiFePO₄软包电池在 1C 倍率下 500 次循环后容量保持率为 86.5%，甚至在 1000 次循环后容量保持率为 70.5%得到了验证。该固态聚合物电解质的阻燃性能通过火焰测试中的优异自熄灭行为（<1 秒）得到了证明。 此外，这个固态聚合物电解质系统为其他电池系统提高负极界面稳定性提供了一种有效的策略。

## Introduction  引言

Lithium-ion batteries, recognized as Nobel Chemistry Prize in 2019, are currently dominant power source for consumer electronics, electric vehicles and grid energy storage [1], [2], [3]. Lithium metal with high theoretical capacity (3860 mAh g−1) and low reduction potential (−3.04 V vs. the standard hydrogen electrode) are concerned as the ultimate anode for next-generation batteries [4,5]. However, the highly reactive lithium metal shows incompatibility with conventional liquid electrolytes by forming notorious lithium dendrites, leading to cell short circuit or even explosion [6]. Alternatively, solid electrolyte owns inherent advantages of reduced lithium dendrites growth, wide operation temperature range and no electrolyte leakage [7,8]. Among solid electrolytes, solid polymer electrolytes (SPEs) possess superior mechanical flexibility and manufacturing scalability, which makes them promising in the practical application such as wearable devices and electric vehicles [9,10]. Nevertheless, the commercial application of SPEs still faces two critical challenges: low Li+ migration number (_t_+) and battery failure with dendrites growth.  
锂离子电池被认为是 2019 年诺贝尔化学奖的研究成果，目前是消费电子、电动汽车和电网储能的主要电源[1]、[2]、[3]。锂金属因其高的理论容量（3860 mAh g−1）和低的还原电位（−3.04 V vs. 标准氢电极）而被视为下一代电池的最终负极材料[4]、[5]。然而，高度反应性的锂金属与传统液态电解质不兼容，会形成著名的锂枝晶，导致电池短路甚至爆炸[6]。相比之下，固态电解质具有减少锂枝晶生长、宽的工作温度范围和无电解质泄漏的固有优势[7]、[8]。在固态电解质中，固态聚合物电解质（SPEs）具有优异的机械柔韧性和制造可扩展性，使其在可穿戴设备和电动汽车等实际应用中具有潜力[9]、[10]。然而，固态聚合物电解质的商业化应用仍面临两个关键挑战：低的锂离子迁移数（tLi+）和枝晶生长导致的电池失效。

Solid polymer electrolytes normally have a Li+ transference number below 0.5, indicating the majority of the total conductivity would be attributed to the anion motion [10,11]. The anions tend to migrate to the opposite site of lithium and accumulate on the electrode surface, leaving a build-up of the concentration gradient and thus limiting the energy density at high discharge rate. These issues can be addressed by immobilizing anions to improve the Li+ transference number in the battery system. Zhao et al. reported a PEO-based polymer electrolyte in which the anions were tethered by PEO chains and LLZTO particles, contributing to a high _t_+ of 0.58 [11]. Similarly, Liu et al. proposed a composite polymer electrolyte by incorporating inorganic ceramics (LLZO) into the polymer framework (LiBFSIE), showing a stunning _t_+as high as 0.94 [12]. These works inhibited the anion diffusion and obtained enhancement value of _t_+, thus it made a regulating Li+ diffusion behavior near the anode side, leading to a stable electrodeposition of lithium ions. However, such an approach can only reduce the lithium dendrite propagation to some extent, as reported dendrite growth even lithium penetration by increasing current density or extending service time.  
固态聚合物电解质通常具有 Li + 迁移数低于 0.5，表明总电导率的主要部分将归因于阴离子的运动[10,11]。阴离子倾向于向锂的相反侧迁移并在电极表面积聚，从而形成浓度梯度并限制了高放电率下的能量密度。这些问题可以通过固定阴离子来解决，以提高电池系统中的 Li + 迁移数。赵等人报道了一种基于 PEO 的聚合物电解质，其中阴离子通过 PEO 链和 LLZTO 颗粒固定，导致高 t + 为 0.58[11]。类似地，刘等人提出了一种复合聚合物电解质，通过将无机陶瓷（LLZO）引入聚合物框架（LiBFSIE）中，显示出令人惊叹的 t + 高达 0.94[12]。这些工作抑制了阴离子的扩散并获得了 t + 的增强值，从而调节了靠近阴极侧的 Li + 扩散行为，导致锂离子的稳定沉积。 然而，这种做法只能在一定程度上抑制锂枝晶的传播，据报道，通过增加电流密度或延长使用时间，锂枝晶甚至会出现穿透现象。

Many strategies have been proposed to eliminate the dendrites growth including constructing three-dimensional host [13,14], optimizing electrolyte with additives [15,16] and engineering artificial protective layer [17,18]. Among them, modification or rebuilding of solid electrolyte interphase (SEI) is a promising approach by which the electrolyte can be spontaneously reduced on the lithium anode surface. The _in-situ_ formed interphase can help tune ionic kinetics with even Li+diffusion path across the SEI layer, which homogenizes the Li+-flux redistribution upon lithium plating/striping, thus inhibit the lithium dendrite nucleation formation [19]. This dendrite nucleation induces local lithium aggregation driven by the strong electric field, whose strength corresponds to the dielectric response beyond the dendrite tip [20]. Therefore, a protective interphase layer with high dielectric permittivity can screen the electric potential and hence help slow down the directed motion of lithium ions near the dendrite nucleation, which can greatly influence the lithium deposition behavior at high current density.  
许多策略已被提出以消除枝晶生长，包括构建三维宿主结构[13,14]、优化电解质添加剂[15,16]和工程化人工保护层[17,18]。其中，通过在锂负极表面自发还原电解质来改进建立固体电解质界面（SEI）是一种有前景的方法。原位形成的界面可以帮助调节离子动力学，甚至在整个 SEI 层中实现均匀的 Li + 扩散路径，从而在锂沉积/剥离过程中均匀化 Li + -通量分布，从而抑制枝晶的形成[19]。这种枝晶的形成是由强电场驱动的局部锂聚集引起的，其强度对应于枝晶尖端的介电响应[20]。因此，具有高介电常数的保护界面层可以屏蔽电势，从而减缓枝晶形成附近锂离子的定向运动，这可以极大地影响在高电流密度下的锂沉积行为。

Herein, we introduced amorphous silicon nitride as an economic and effective additive in a polyvinylidene fluoride (PVDF)-based solid polymer electrolyte. As shown in Fig. 1a. silicon nitride with amorphous structure is composed of abundant dangling bonds (immobilized free radicals, N3![triple bond](https://sdfestaticassets-us-east-1.sciencedirectassets.com/shared-assets/55/entities/tbnd.gif)Si·, Si2=N·), which is responsible for the high dielectric permittivity property [21,22]. Moreover, silicon nitride can be reduced by lithium anode and forms lithium silicon nitride (LiSi_x_N_y_), which is well-known as a Li-ion conductor, enabling a rapid Li+ diffusion on the anode surface [23,24]. Lithium difluoro(oxalato)borate (LiDFOB) with relatively high HUMO level tends to be oxidized and forms a robust cathode electrolyte interphase (CEI), while the lithium bis(tri-fluoromethylsulfonyl)imide (LiTFSI) inclines to be reduced and forms a stable solid electrolyte interphase (SEI), thus this dual-salt system could contribute a robust and conformal CEI/SEI on the electrodes [25]. As a result, the as-prepared PVDF/Si3N4 solid polymer electrolyte exhibits both high ionic conductivity of 5.7 × 10−4 S cm−1 and high Li+ transference number (_t_+ = 0.53) at room temperature. Paired with this solid polymer electrolyte, the symmetric Li//Li cell can be stably cycled at a high current density of 1.0 mA cm−2 over 250 h, and the practical application of this solid polymer electrolyte is also verified by Li//LiFePO4 pouch cell in 1000 cycles with capacity retention of 70.5%. Additionally, the fire retardant of PVDF/Si3N4 electrolyte is also demonstrated due to the highly thermal stability of silicon nitride ceramic. Moreover, this work also provides an effective strategy for improving the interfacial stability with high dielectric constant protective layer, leading to a dendrite-free interface in other energy storage systems such as sodium battery and zinc battery.  
herein，我们引入了无定形硅氮化物作为聚偏氟乙烯（PVDF）基固态聚合物电解质中的经济有效的添加剂。如图 1a 所示，无定形结构的硅氮化物由丰富的悬挂键（固定自由基，N 3 ![triple bond](https://sdfestaticassets-us-east-1.sciencedirectassets.com/shared-assets/55/entities/tbnd.gif) Si·，Si 2 =N·）组成，这赋予了其高介电常数的性质[21,22]。此外，硅氮化物可以被锂负极还原并形成锂硅氮化物（LiSi x N y ），众所周知这是一种 Li 离子导体，能够使锂 + 在负极表面快速扩散[23,24]。六氟(氧乙酸)硼酸锂（LiDFOB）具有相对较高的 HUMO 水平，容易被氧化并形成坚固的正极电解质界面（CEI），而二（三氟甲基磺酰基）亚胺锂（LiTFSI）倾向于被还原并形成稳定的固体电解质界面（SEI），因此这种双盐系统能够为电极提供坚固且贴合的 CEI/SEI[25]。因此，所制备的 PVDF/Si 3 N 4 固态聚合物电解质表现出高离子电导率（5.7×10 −4 S cm −1 ）和高锂 + 迁移数（t + =0）。53) 在室温下。与这种固态聚合物电解质配对，对称的 Li//Li 电池可以在 1.0 mA cm⁻²的高电流密度下稳定循环超过 250 小时，并且通过 1000 次循环的 Li//LiFePO₄软包电池，也验证了这种固态聚合物电解质的实际应用，容量保持率为 70.5%。此外，由于硅氮化物陶瓷的高热稳定性，PVDF/Si 3 N 4 电解质的阻燃性能也得到了证明。此外，这项工作还提供了一种有效策略，通过使用高介电常数保护层来提高界面稳定性，从而在钠电池和锌电池等其他储能系统中实现无枝晶界面。

## Conclusions  结论

In summary, solid polymer electrolyte made of PVDF and amorphous Si3N4 was verified to have excellent electrochemical performance. The amorphous silicon nitride with high dielectric permittivity enhances the uniform lithium electrodeposition by screening electric potential at high current density. Moreover, the reduction product from the _in-situ_ reaction between lithium anode and silicon nitride is beneficial to interfacial chemistry, especially the _in-situ_ formed LiSi2N3 shows a better Li+  
总结而言，由 PVDF 和无定形 Si 3 N 4 组成的固态聚合物电解质被验证具有出色的电化学性能。高介电常数的无定形硅氮化物通过在高电流密度下屏蔽电位，促进锂均匀沉积。此外，锂负极与硅氮化物原位反应的还原产物对界面化学有利，尤其是原位形成的 LiSi 2 N 3 表现出更好的 Li + 。
